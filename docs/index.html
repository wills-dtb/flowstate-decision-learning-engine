What counts as cash evidence?

  • Overtime payments actually reduced
  • Contractor hours or fees removed
  • A supplier contract reduced or cancelled
  • An approved budget line removed

What does not count as cash?

  • People simply finishing work sooner
  • Time redirected to higher-value tasks
  • A theoretical hourly-rate calculation
  • Future savings without financial proof
